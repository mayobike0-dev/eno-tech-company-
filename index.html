03 — Custom Measurements

Measurements That Work Your Way.

Every designer measures differently. SEAVARE allows designers to create measurement systems that match the way they work. Designers can create measurement templates, add custom fields, rename fields, reorder fields, delete fields, and create different templates for different garments.

Men's Shirt Women's Dress Trousers Suit Traditional Wear Bridal Custom
Male Female Unisex cm in
SEAVARE V1 uses manual measurement entry. It does not include photo measurement, video measurement, AI body scanning, or automatic body measurement.
How Measurements Work

Measurements live with your designer.

Customers don't have a personal measurement-storage area, and can't create measurement slots or give designers free measurement capacity. Measurements belong to the designer's customer-management system — a designer can choose to share a measurement record with a customer, and that customer can view what's been shared.

  • Measurements belong to the designer's customer-management system
  • Designers can share a measurement record with a customer
  • Customers can view measurements shared by their designer
